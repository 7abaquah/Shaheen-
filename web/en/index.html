About

What is Shaheen?

A Saudi group bringing its digital services into one platform, with SHN as the unit of use and governance.

We state what the contracts define, say openly what is still to be decided, and make no promise of returns.

SHN token

A BEP-20 token on BNB Chain with a total supply of 1,000,000,000 SHN; 1% is burned on every presale purchase.

DAO governance

Major decisions, including raising the presale hardcap, go to a vote of SHN holders, followed by a 48-hour timelock before they take effect.

Compute nodes

Solar-powered nodes in Makkah, Jeddah and Riyadh. Their rewards are paid from the pre-defined EcoSystem allocation.

Mining

Compute nodes ("mining")

At Shaheen, "mining" means running real compute nodes. Their rewards are paid from the EcoSystem allocation defined in advance in the smart contract.

SHN is not produced by proof of work (PoW). It is a BEP-20 token with a fixed supply of 1,000,000,000 SHN; rewards are paid from an existing balance, not from newly created tokens.
