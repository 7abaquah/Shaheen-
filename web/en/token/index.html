Presale terms

  • Rate: 238 SHN per 1 USDT.
  • Burn: 1% is burned on every purchase.
  • Referral: a 5% reward, single level only — no multi-level structure.
  • Participation limits: 50 to 10,000 USDT per wallet.
  • Hardcap: 2,400,000 USDT, raisable only by a DAO vote.

Governance (DAO)

SHN holders decide major changes through on-chain proposals and votes, followed by a timelock so everyone can review a decision before it takes effect.

Proposal threshold10,000 SHN
Voting delay1 day
Voting period1 week

  4. QuorumA 4% quorum is required for the result to stand.

Security and audit

The contracts are at version 2 (v2) and have been tested. An independent third-party audit is not yet complete; we will publish the full report when it is issued. Until then, treat the contracts as not independently audited.

Contract addresses: [Published after launch]. To report a vulnerability, see the security and responsible disclosure page.

Risks

  • Price volatility: the value of SHN can rise or fall sharply, and you may lose the full amount.
  • Smart-contract risk: the contracts may contain defects, and the independent audit is not yet complete.
  • Rewards are not guaranteed: staking and node rewards are variable and limited to what the pre-funded pool holds.
  • Regulatory risk: digital-asset rules change and may affect availability or use of the token in your country.
  • Liquidity: a market to sell SHN may not exist, or may be limited.
  • Governance decisions: a DAO vote may change parameters such as the presale hardcap.
  • Transfer errors: on-chain transactions are final; sending to a wrong address or over another network results in loss of funds.
  • Fraud and impersonation: beware of accounts and addresses impersonating Shaheen; official addresses are published only on this website.
