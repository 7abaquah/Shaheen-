Data controller

Shaheen is the controller of personal data collected through this website. Registered entity name, commercial registration number and national address: [TBD]

Data we collect

We collect only what you enter in the forms, plus limited technical data to protect them:

  • Updates form: name, email, mobile number (optional), message language, interests.
  • Plan request form: plan, organisation name, contact person, email, mobile number, preferred payment method, number of users and notes (both optional). We do not collect card details.
  • Investor interest form: full name, email, mobile number, country of residence, investor type, organisation name, approximate interest size, self-declaration of qualified-investor status, message, page language.
  • Consent record: the version of this policy you accepted (currently 2026-11) and the time of consent.
  • IP address: used only to limit repeated submissions (10 per hour per form) and stored as a salted SHA-256 hash, never in its original form.

This website uses no tracking or advertising cookies, no analytics tools, and does not store form data in your browser.

Third-party services on this website

  • Cloudflare Turnstile: a verification tool that processes browser and device signals to confirm the sender is not an automated program; it is subject to Cloudflare's privacy policy.
  • Cloudflare Pages: hosting and delivery of the website.
  • Google Fonts: your browser loads fonts from Google's servers, which receive your IP address and standard browser information.

Purposes of processing

  • Sending updates matching the interests you chose.
  • Reviewing plan requests, contacting you, and issuing quotations and invoices.
  • Reviewing investor registrations of interest, contacting you about them, and requesting identity verification (KYC) later, once regulatory requirements are complete.
  • Protecting the forms from abuse and automated messages.
  • Complying with the laws of the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia.

We do not use your data for any other purpose without your new consent.

Legal basis

We process your data on the basis of the explicit consent you give when submitting a form, under the Saudi Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL) and its regulations. You can withdraw consent at any time; withdrawal does not affect processing carried out before it.

Your rights

The PDPL gives you the following rights:

  • To be informed of what data we collect and why — as set out in this policy.
  • Access to the data we hold about you.
  • A copy of your data in a clear format.
  • Correction, completion or updating of your data.
  • Destruction of your data once it is no longer needed.
  • Withdrawal of consent at any time.
  • Complaint to the competent authority, the Saudi Data & AI Authority (SDAIA).

To exercise any right, contact us using the details below; we will respond within the statutory period. We may ask for proof of identity before acting on a request.

Data security

We protect data with encrypted connections (HTTPS) and a strict content security policy, restrict the admin interface to a secret key, record every admin change in an audit log, and store IP addresses only as hashes.
