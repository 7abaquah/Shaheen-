Reporting a vulnerability

If you discover a security vulnerability in Shaheen's systems, please report it to us privately before any public disclosure.

Reporting email: [TBD] — the authoritative contact details are always in /.well-known/security.txt.

Please include:

  • A description of the vulnerability and its potential impact.
  • The affected URL, component or contract.
  • Steps to reproduce, with a short proof of concept if possible.
  • How we can reach you.

Scope

  • This website.
  • The API that receives the forms.
  • The web app (PWA).
  • The SHN smart contracts, once their official addresses are published.

Out of scope

  • Denial-of-service (DoS/DDoS) attacks or any testing that degrades our systems.
  • Social engineering or phishing of staff or users, and physical testing.
  • Mass form submissions or spam.
  • Automated scanner output without demonstrated impact.
  • Vulnerabilities in third-party services such as Cloudflare and Google — report them to those providers.

Testing rules

  • Do not access, modify or delete other people's data; use only test data you created.
  • If you reach personal data unintentionally, stop immediately, tell us and keep no copy.
  • Do not exploit the issue beyond what is needed to demonstrate it.
  • Do not disclose publicly before a fix and an agreed disclosure date.

Our commitment

  • We acknowledge receipt and keep you informed of progress. Response and fix timelines: [TBD]
  • We will not take action against researchers who follow these rules in good faith.
  • Bug bounty programme: [TBD]

What this website applies

  • Mandatory encrypted connections (HTTPS with HSTS).
  • A strict Content Security Policy that blocks any unapproved script.
  • No trackers or ads, and no form data stored in the browser.
  • Cloudflare Turnstile verification and rate limiting on every form.
  • The website accepts no funds.

Protect yourself

  • We will never ask for your wallet's recovery (seed) phrase or private key.
  • Official contract addresses are published only on this website after launch; any address circulating before then is not official.
  • We do not request transfers through private messages or social media.
  • Check the domain name in the address bar before entering any data.
